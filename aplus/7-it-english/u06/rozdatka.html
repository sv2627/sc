Sub-routines and patterns

Довідка

Sub-routine

a separate algorithm that has a name and can be called from other parts of an algorithm. It runs its steps and then returns control.

The symbol

rectangle with two lines at the sides — to start the sub-routine and to call it. The identifier (name) is the same in both places. At the end — STOP or RETURN.

Pattern

a repeating sequence of data, values, text, images or signs. Find the repetition → put it in a sub-routine → write once, call many times.
