Flowchart symbols: input and output. Sub-routines in a hurdle race

Keep this

input · output

The symbol is a parallelogram. An input is data from outside that enters into the system. An output is data or information that leaves the system.

a sub-routine, many times

The main algorithm calls a sub-routine. Each sub-routine ends with Return. It keeps the main algorithm smaller, simpler and easier to follow.

the hurdle race

10 hurdles at an equal distance. Sprint 7 steps · hurdle · 9 times: sprint 4 steps and hurdle · sprint 7 steps · finish line.
