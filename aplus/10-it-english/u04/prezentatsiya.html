Calculating the size of an image file

«Although most computers will calculate the size of a file for you, you need to be able to show that you can do this manually for the exam. This is just for an image file and a sound file.» — с. 25

number of images

the number of images in the file

resolution

the resolution of the image

colour depth

the colour depth of the image

width of image × height of image × colour depth of image × number of images in file
🧮
Worked example · с. 26

10 images · 100 × 150 · 8-bit colour

1100 × 150 = 15 000 — there are 15 000 pixels in the image
215 000 × 8 = 120 000 — there are 120 000 bits in each image
3120 000 × 10 = 1 200 000 — there are 1 200 000 bits in the image file
41 200 000 ÷ 8 = 150 000 bytes — бо в байті 8 бітів
5150 000 ÷ 1024 = 146.5 KiB — to 1 decimal place
Questions 1–2 Coursebook с. 26

Question 1

How many times would you need to divide the answer in bytes by 1024 to find the answer in Gibibytes?

Спершу відповідь, потім клац.

3 times. bytes → KiB → MiB → GiB: ÷ 1024 ÷ 1024 ÷ 1024.

Question 2

How many pixels would there be in an image that has a resolution of 1000 × 1500?

Спершу відповідь, потім клац.

1 500 000 pixels. Width × height — як крок 1 прикладу: 100 × 150 = 15 000.

SKILLS FOCUS 1.3 · с. 26

Calculating the size of a sound file

length

the length of the soundtrack

sample rate

the sample rate of the soundtrack

sample resolution

the sample resolution of the soundtrack

sample rate × sample resolution × length of soundtrack
🔗
sample rate і sample resolution — слова підрозділу 1.3. Тепер з них виходить число.
🧮
Worked example · с. 26–27

30 seconds · 44 100 Hz · 8 bits

144 100 × 8 = 352 800 — there are 352 800 bits per second
2352 800 × 30 = 10 584 000 — there are 10 584 000 bits in the sound file
310 584 000 ÷ 8 = 1 323 000 bytes
41 323 000 ÷ 1024 ÷ 1024 = 1.3 MiB — to 1 decimal place
MiB — це двічі ÷ 1024: байти → KiB → MiB.
