Linear search in sentences

1 Look at the first card.

2 If it is the value you are looking for, stop.

3 If it is not, put the card back face down.

4 Turn over the next card.

5 Repeat until you find the required card.

6 If all cards have been searched and the value is not found, stop.

Two endings

found — потрібне значення знайдено, стоп

not found — перевірили всі картки, його немає

Swap and test

Swap with another group. Test with cards: the value is there · the value is not there. Make corrections.

Далі — олівцем. Комп'ютер не потрібен, можна в сховищі
