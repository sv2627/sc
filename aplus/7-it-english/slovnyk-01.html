3 · Phrases · Фрази, які ми казали на уроці

  • This flowchart takes two numbers, then subtracts the second input from the first, and outputs the result. Наш зразок речення. Слова до нього: adds · subtracts · multiplies · divides · from · by.
  • This flowchart outputs the larger number. Ця схема виводить більше число.
  • The error was in the … because … Помилка була в …, тому що … (як ми пояснювали виправлення)
  • We changed the output. Ми змінили вивід. (що саме змінили під нову вимогу)
  • «Works» and «correct» are not the same. Correct means it meets the requirement. «Працює» і «правильно» — різні речі. Правильно = відповідає вимозі.
  • IF your favourite colour is red stand up, otherwise sit on the floor. Команда, яку виконував клас: одна команда — дві різні дії.
  • IS mark >= 7? · IS height >= 140? Так пишеться питання всередині ромба.
  • the question · the true data flow · the false data flow Три підписи, якими ми позначали схему.

4 · Questions and answers · Питання й базові відповіді

1. What is an algorithm?
A precise order of steps that finishes the job.
2. What is a flowchart?
One way to write an algorithm: shapes and arrows. The shape shows what kind of step it is.
3. Name the flowchart symbols.
start / stop — oval · input / output — the sloping one · process — rectangle · decision — diamond · data flow — arrow.
4. What is the purpose of the rectangle symbol?
It is a process: the computer works with the data inside, and the person does not see it.
5. Why might you change an algorithm? (four reasons)
It contains an error, or bug · it needs to do something extra · its purpose has changed · it is being adapted from a flowchart used for another task.
6. A flowchart works. Is it correct?
Not always. Correct means it meets the requirement. Read the requirement first.
7. What is selection?
Choosing one way out of two. In a flowchart it is the diamond — the decision.
8. How many arrows come out of a decision?
Exactly two: true / yes and false / no. Both arrows are labelled.
9. What can the answer to a condition be?
Only true or false. Not «almost», not «sometimes».
10. Is 4 > 5? Is 3 <= 3? Is 19 != 19?
false · true · false. Three is not less than three, but it is equal — so 3 <= 3 is true.
11. Which two operators are new, and not used in maths?
== (equal to) and != (not equal to). The ! means «not».
12. Entry is allowed from 18 years and older. Which sign?
IS age >= 18? — because 18 exactly is allowed too.
13. Your classmate drew a diamond with three arrows: yes, no, I don’t know. Why is this wrong?
A condition has only two answers, so a decision has exactly two data flows.
