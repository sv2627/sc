2 · Phrases · Фрази, які ми казали на уроці

  • Computers represent all data in binary. Комп'ютер подає всі дані у двійковому вигляді.
  • 1 = on, 0 = off. Одиниця — струм іде, нуль — струму немає.
  • There are ___ black pixels. Тут ___ чорних пікселів. (так ми рахували спрайта)
  • 2 bits make 4 colours. Два біти дають чотири кольори: 00 · 01 · 10 · 11.
  • 1 byte = 8 bits. Один байт — вісім бітів.
  • byte → kilobyte → megabyte → gigabyte → terabyte Сходинка одиниць: кожна наступна більша приблизно в тисячу разів.
  • Shade the squares. Зафарбуй клітинки (за кодом).
  • Which storage media would you choose? Why this one? Який носій ти обереш? Чому саме цей?

3 · Questions and answers · Питання й базові відповіді

1. What is a bit?
A bit is one binary digit: 0 or 1.
2. How many states does a switch have?
Two: on or off.
3. What is inside a CPU?
Billions of tiny switches. They are called transistors.
4. What is a pixel?
One small square of an image — the smallest part of a picture.
5. What is a bitmap?
An image made from many pixels.
6. How many colours can 2 bits make?
Four: 00, 01, 10, 11.
7. How many bits are in one byte?
Eight.
8. A row has 8 cells and each cell is 2 bits. How many bytes is the row?
8 × 2 = 16 bits = 2 bytes.
9. Name five storage media.
SD card · micro SD card · flash drive · hard disk drive · solid state disk.
10. What does capacity mean?
How much data a storage medium can hold.
11. What is transfer speed?
How fast a file is copied to the storage medium and back.
12. Why are capacity and speed not the same?
Capacity is how much it holds. Transfer speed is how fast it writes.
13. Name three things that change your choice of storage.
For example portability, security and cost.
14. You need to bring holiday photos to school. Which storage media, and why?
A flash drive — because of portability.
15. All the family films stay at home. Which one, and why?
A hard disk drive — capacity and cost.
16. A game must start quickly. Which one, and why?
An SSD — because of speed.
17. Two SD cards look the same. Why can one hold a thousand times more?
Capacity is not visible. You read it on the card itself or in the properties on the computer.
